Desargues' Theorem and Projective Planes

Published: Fri 25 September 2026 | Category: Math

In projective geometry, two triangles are in perspective axially if and only if they are in perspective centrally.

Let the two triangles be $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle A'B'C'$. If the lines $AA'$, $BB'$, and $CC'$ concur at a center $O$:

$$P = BC \cap B'C', \quad Q = CA \cap C'A', \quad R = AB \cap A'B'$$

Then $P$, $Q$, and $R$ lie on a common line (the axis of perspectivity).
