Cyclic Quadrilaterals and Ptolemy's Theorem

Published: Fri 25 September 2026 | Category: Math

Here is a short note on cyclic quadrilaterals.

Theorem (Ptolemy). For any cyclic quadrilateral with vertices $A, B, C, D$ in order: $$AC \cdot BD = AB \cdot CD + BC \cdot DA$$
The proof follows from constructing a point $E$ on $AC$ such that $\angle ABE = \angle DBC$ and using similar triangles.
