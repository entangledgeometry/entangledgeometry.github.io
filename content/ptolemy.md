Title: Cyclic Quadrilaterals and Ptolemy's Theorem
Date: 2026-09-25
Category: Math
Tags: geometry, olympiad

Here is a short note on cyclic quadrilaterals.

<div class="theorem">
<span class="theorem-title">Theorem (Ptolemy).</span>
For any cyclic quadrilateral with vertices $A, B, C, D$ in order:
$$AC \cdot BD = AB \cdot CD + BC \cdot DA$$
</div>

<div class="proof">
The proof follows from constructing a point $E$ on $AC$ such that $\angle ABE = \angle DBC$ and using similar triangles.
</div>
